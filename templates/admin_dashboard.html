How Return Insight AI works

The customer-selected reason is the starting point, not the confirmed actual reason. The AI module examines submitted text and images to estimate a likely reason and identify supporting or conflicting evidence. Staff must inspect the case and record the confirmed reason separately. Evidence strength does not automatically establish whether the customer's reason is correct, and this system does not decide return eligibility.

1. Customer submission 2. AI evidence assessment 3. Prioritize uncertain cases 4. Staff verification 5. Compare reasons
